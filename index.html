How to play

  • Click or tap a cell to open it. Numbers count the mines around it.
  • Right-click, long-press or switch to Flag mode to mark a mine.
  • Press a number whose mines are all flagged to open its neighbours.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Enter or Space opens, F flags.
